Wildbook 10.11: Fast Matching in Seconds, Match Visualization, AI Co-scientist, and New Match Results Page
August 2026
Wildbook 10.11 rebuilds MiewID matching around a fast vector database, so matches against large catalogs come back in seconds. It also adds PairX match visualizations developed with MIT's Beery Lab, a redesigned Match Result page, and data access through ChatGPT or Claude.
read more

New Algorithm: "MiewID In-depth" for Individual Animal Re-ID
March 2026
MiewID In-depth pairs the MiewID v4.1 model with LightGlue feature matching to re-rank its top 50 predictions. It improved top-1 accuracy for 69 of 93 species, including Iberian lynx, ocelots, spotted hyenas, and trout.
read more

MiewID v4: The Latest AI for Individual Animal Re-ID
January 2026
MiewID v4 was trained on 40% more data than v3, covering about 90 species, among them elephants, chital and sika deer, tiger sharks, and seadragons. Average top-1 accuracy rose to 78%.
