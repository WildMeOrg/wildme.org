​Supporters

Our supporters

Wild Me is grateful to all our sponsors that help make conservation happen. Join us in celebrating our supporters, whose generosity and direction make our work possible.
Become A Sponsor

Patrick J. McGovern Foundation

Featured sponsor
The Patrick J. McGovern Foundation is a 21st century philanthropy advancing data and AI for social good. The foundation supports Wild Me Lab’s AI for Giant Sequoias work, which uses satellite AI to monitor individual giant sequoias. Visit mcgovern.org.

The Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation

Featured sponsor
The Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation fosters path-breaking scientific discovery, environmental conservation, patient care improvements and preservation of the special character of the Bay Area. Visit Moore.org and follow @MooreFound.

​Paul G. Allen Family Foundation

Featured partner
The Seattle-based Paul G. Allen Family Foundation, founded by philanthropists Jody Allen and the late Paul Allen, is pleased to partner with Wild Me to improve wildlife survey accuracy and reliability. The foundation supports frontline partners around the world working to preserve ocean health, protect wildlife, combat climate change and strengthen communities.

H2O.ai

​Featured sponsor
On August 13, 2018, H2O.ai became a sponsor of Wildbook in celebration of World Elephant Day. H2O.ai's continued investment helps Wildbook support its staff, accelerating the conservation of endangered species with AI.

Microsoft

Featured sponsor
On June 14, 2018, Wild Me became a featured partner of Microsoft's AI for Earth program. This deep level of investment and engagement has enabled Wildbook to more effectively and efficiently use AI to combat extinction. Wild Me joined the Microsoft for Startups program in 2020.

​NVIDIA

​Featured sponsor
Wild Me is a member of NVIDIA's Inception Incubator program and has received hardware donations from NVIDIA.

​Marine Megafauna Foundation

Featured partner
Marine Megafauna Foundation partnered with Wild Me to create MantaMatcher, the Wildbook for mantas and other rays.

​National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)

​Featured partner
NOAA partners with Wild Me to improve Flukebook.

Giraffe Conservation Foundation

Featured partner
The Giraffe Conservation Foundation partnered with Wild Me to create GiraffeSpotter.org, the Wildbook for Giraffe.

​SeadragonSearch

​Featured partner
SeadragonSearch partnered with Wild Me to create SeadragonSearch.org, the Wildbook for Seadragons.
